The Toaster Showdown!

Tonight's host is Toastmaster Crispin, a toaster with the confidence of six toasters. He has an order ticket for every slot: the little badge in the corner shows how toasted each slice must be.

  • Pulling a lever toasts that slice and its neighbors (left, right, up, down) one shade darker.
  • Toast past the darkest shade and it wraps back to raw. Caine calls it "Un-Toasting." Science is baffled.
  • Match every badge to win the round. Beat par for three stars.
  • Stuck? The Oracle (a gumball machine; do not call her that) trades gumballs for prophecies.

Also, Jax has been fitted with a Niceness Patch. He's fine. He's being very nice about it.
